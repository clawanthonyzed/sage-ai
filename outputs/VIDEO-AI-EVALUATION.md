## 3. 🏆 Proposed pick (for Anthony's review)

| Rank | Engine | Role for Sage | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥇 | **HeyGen Creator (A$41.66/month)** | Every talking-to-camera clip | Lip-sync to a supplied voice is its core job, it works from one approved Sage photo, and its cost per talking minute is the lowest or near-lowest |
| 🥈 | **Hedra Creator (A$43.10/month)** | Backup talking head | Clear per-second pricing (A$2.87/min) and audio-driven faces; test against HeyGen before paying for either |
| 🎥 | **Higgsfield Starter (A$27.30/month)** | Lifestyle b-roll: beach walks, kitchen, golden-hour shots with camera moves | Best cinematic camera and photoreal look; not the strongest for long lip-synced talking |
