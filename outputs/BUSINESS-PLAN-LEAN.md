## 🛡️ 0. Guardrails (every asset, every time)

| Rule | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| 🚫 Zero "midwife" claims | Sage is never a midwife, nurse or any registered health practitioner. These are protected titles under the National Law that AHPRA administers. "Midwife" may only mean the reader's own carer ("ask your midwife") |
| 🤖 AI disclosure on all visuals | Instagram/Facebook: switch on Meta's "AI info" label on every post with a Sage image. Gumroad and site images carry a small "AI-created character" note |
| 🪪 Always "virtual" | The brand line says Virtual Birth Doula. Never imply Sage is a real person |
| 🩺 No medical advice | Every factual claim cites RANZCOG, WHO or healthdirect. Every product carries: "General information only, not medical advice. Talk to your midwife, GP or obstetrician." |
| ⏸️ Posting hold | Instagram posting still waits for Anthony's go-ahead + account access (hold since 2026-10-11) |
